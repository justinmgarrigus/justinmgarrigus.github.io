Introduction

Hello! My name is Justin. I’m a PhD student at the University of Texas at Austin currently studying computer architecture. My advisor is Dr. Lizy Kurian John, and my work is with the Laboratory of Computer Architecture. I’m currently interested in creating hardware-aware agentic AI systems.

Previously, my Master’s thesis is (1) an exploration of deep-learning compilers, (2) an L2-cache microarchitecture for reducing duplicated values in lowered im2col convolution, and (3) an analysis of distributed GPU systems evaluating LLM workloads, and was advised by Dr. Hui Zhao at the University of North Texas. My Bachelor’s thesis is a detailed investigation of programming languages, syntax vs. semantics, and the introduction of a custom programming language maximizing expressibility of any programming problem, advised by Dr. Barrett Bryant.

My full CV can be found here. You can contact me at justingarrigus@utexas.edu.
